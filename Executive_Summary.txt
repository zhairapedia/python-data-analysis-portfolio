EXECUTIVE SUMMARY Retail Sales & Customer Insights – Data Analytics
Capstone

• This project analyzes seven related retail/e-commerce datasets:
customers, products, orders, order items, payments, shipments, and
returns. • The objective is to understand sales performance, customer
purchasing behavior, product contribution, and operational activity. •
The datasets are connected using customer ID, product ID, and order ID
relationships. • Sales performance is evaluated using gross revenue, net
revenue, fulfilled orders, and average order value (AOV). • Monthly
revenue analysis is used to identify changes in sales over time. •
Product, category, region, and city comparisons are used to identify key
sales contributors. • Customer purchasing behavior is examined through
new, repeat, and single-order customer classifications. • Payment
methods and payment statuses are reviewed to understand transaction
activity. • Estimated gross profit and discount patterns are examined to
provide additional commercial context. • Shipment, cancellation, and
return-request data are explored to review operational outcomes. • An
interactive Streamlit dashboard provides filters for month, region,
sales channel, and product category. • Jupyter Notebook outputs and
exported charts document the calculations and support the business
findings. • Final insights and recommendations should be based on
verified notebook and dashboard results.
