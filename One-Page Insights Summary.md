# One-Page Insights Summary

## Retail Sales \& Customer Insights -- Data Analytics Capstone

### Project objective

Analyze seven related retail/e-commerce datasets to evaluate sales
performance, product and geographic contribution, customer purchasing
behavior, payment activity, profitability, and operational outcomes.

### Scope and approach

The analysis uses customers, products, orders, order items, payments,
shipments, and returns data. The tables are connected through customer,
product, and order identifiers. The dashboard provides Month, Region,
Sales Channel, and Product Category filters. Metrics include gross
revenue, net revenue, fulfilled orders, average order value, monthly
revenue, product/category/location revenue, estimated gross profit,
repeat customer rate, payment activity, cancellations, and return
requests.

### Three priority insights

1\. Sales Trend: August generated ₱239,638 in revenue.  

Business Meaning: Indicates an opportunity to improve sales through targeted promotions and better inventory planning.

2\. Product and Location Performance: Toys led category sales at ₱750,760, while Region III generated the highest regional revenue at ₱950,979.  

Business Meaning: Focus on popular products and strengthen marketing and distribution in high-performing locations.

3\. Customer Purchasing Behavior:\*\* Repeat customers accounted for 74.9% of the customer base.  

Business Meaning: Strong repeat purchasing highlights the importance of customer loyalty programs and retention strategies to sustain revenue growth.

Recommendations

* Prioritize inventory and product visibility for products/categories
with strong verified revenue contribution.
* Use customer retention campaigns where the repeat-purchase analysis
identifies an opportunity.
* Review regional performance to guide targeted marketing and
distribution decisions.
* Monitor discounts and estimated gross profit together when
evaluating promotions.
* Investigate failed/pending payments and recurring delivery or return
issues where supported by the results.
